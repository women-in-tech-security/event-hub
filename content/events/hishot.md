**Women In Tech Security (WTS)** partnered with the **Hishot 2024 Seminar** organized by the Informatics Engineering Association at **Binus University**.  

The event, themed **"PROTECT (Preventing Risks, Overcoming Threats, Empowering Cybersecurity Talents),"** featured a series of seminars and workshops focused on cybersecurity.  

As a media partner, **WTS supported the event** to enhance cybersecurity awareness and education, aligning with our mission to **empower and inspire future cybersecurity talents**.
