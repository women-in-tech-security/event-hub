Women In Tech Security (WTS) launched our Volunteer Recruitment Program for 2024/2025, a cornerstone initiative aimed at fostering leadership skills and empowering young Indonesians to become future cybersecurity leaders. Successful candidates will have the opportunity to address emerging cybersecurity issues and contribute to our mission. This program seeks individuals committed to making significant social contributions, nurturing community leaders, and developing essential soft skills for success in the cybersecurity industry. Passionate about creating a safer digital world? Join our diverse and inclusive WTS volunteer team!
