WTS participated in a Focus Group Discussion (FGD) organized by Badan Siber dan Sandi Negara (BSSN) regarding the development of community guidelines for handling negative content in cyberspace. The FGD aimed to discuss cybersecurity issues related to social threats in Indonesia, particularly negative content, and to identify the role of communities in addressing these challenges. The session also focused on developing guidelines to help communities effectively manage and respond to negative content online.
