On September 28, 2024, WTS, in collaboration with XyberXecurity and Jogja Cyber Security (JCS), hosted a webinar titled "Don't Be a Victim: Boost Your Security Awareness Against Social Engineering and Phishing." The event aimed to raise awareness about the dangers of social engineering and phishing attacks, providing participants with essential knowledge and tips on how to recognize and protect themselves from these common cyber threats. The webinar targeted individuals interested in improving their cybersecurity awareness, regardless of their technical background.
