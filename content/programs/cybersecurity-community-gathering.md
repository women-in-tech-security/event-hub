Women In Tech Security (WTS) hosted an informal networking event for tech and cybersecurity professionals in Sanur, Bali. Held on June 7, 2024, the event provided a relaxed environment for attendees from cybersecurity and tech backgrounds, including participants from Laos and Malaysia, to connect over coffee and snacks. This gathering aimed to foster collaboration and idea-sharing among like-minded individuals in the industry.
