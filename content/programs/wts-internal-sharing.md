Women In Tech Security (WTS) held a series of Internal Sharing Sessions in 2024, covering key cybersecurity topics such as malware response, career opportunities in the industry, OSINT and privacy protection, and web penetration testing for ethical hacking. These sessions aimed to equip members with practical skills and insights to tackle cybersecurity challenges and strengthen their expertise.
