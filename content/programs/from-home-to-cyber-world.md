On August 27, 2024, WTS, in collaboration with Jogja Cyber Security (JCS), hosted an event titled "From Home to the Cyber World: How Women Can Become Cybersecurity Experts," aimed at empowering women to pursue careers in cybersecurity. The event focused on how women can leverage opportunities in the field, regardless of their background, and encouraged them to develop the necessary skills to succeed. The target audience included women interested in starting or advancing their careers in cybersecurity, whether from technical or non-technical backgrounds, as well as those seeking to strengthen their roles in the industry.
