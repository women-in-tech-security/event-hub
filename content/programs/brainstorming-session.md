Women In Tech Security (WTS) participated in a brainstorming session with BINUS University to assist a lecturer in developing an article on the impact of women in cybersecurity practices, policymaking, and knowledge production, particularly in the context of the Global South. This article will be submitted to the Journal on Policing, Intelligence and Counterterrorism, The session focused on discussing women in cybersecurity issues to gather insights for the article's development.
