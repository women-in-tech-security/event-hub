On June 28, 2024, Women In Tech Security (WTS) collaborated with Anon Cyber Team (ACT) and HIMA Universitas Muhammadiyah Cirebon (UMC) to host a National Cybersecurity Webinar. Speakers from ACT, WTS, and the cybersecurity community covered cyber forensics, malware analysis and protection, and general security awareness, aiming to educate participants and enhance cybersecurity practices against evolving threats.
