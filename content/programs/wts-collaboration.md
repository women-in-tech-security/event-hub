Women In Tech Security (WTS) has partnered with Orang Siber Indonesia for content collaboration, including content feeds, Instagram Live sessions, and an upcoming podcast series. This collaboration aims to raise awareness and share valuable insights on cybersecurity topics, reaching a broader audience. By leveraging social media platforms, WTS and Orang Siber Indonesia collaborate to deliver engaging and informative content. Through this partnership, we can reach a broader audience and contribute to the growing cybersecurity discourse within the community.
