On June 29, 2024, WTS held an online workshop titled "Protecting Children Online From Cyber Threats," addressing the need for stronger protection measures and digital safety education due to growing cyber threats targeting children. Featuring speakers from WTS, SEJIWA Foundation, and Orang Siber Indonesia, the workshop covered topics such as cyber threats to children, parental monitoring best practices, and tools for controlling children's online activities, and was attended by parents, students, office workers, and government officials.
