Frequently Ask Question

What is Women in Tech Security (WTS) and its purpose? ➕
Women In Tech Security (WTS) is a community organization dedicated to raising cybersecurity awareness, supporting career development in cybersecurity, and building a collaborative community. Our mission is to create a safe and inclusive digital environment by educating the public, promoting diversity, and encouraging active participation.
What kind of events does WTS organize and how can I participate? ➕
WTS organizes various kinds of events and initiatives both online or offline including mentoring, workshops, panel discussions, networking meetups, social media campaigns, and many more. You can participate by registering through links on our social media pages, website, or event announcements. Stay updated by following us on Instagram @womenintechsecurity_id, subscribing to our newsletter, and regularly visiting our website. We also share updates through our social media channels.
Who can become a member of WTS and how can I volunteer for WTS? ➕
WTS is open to anyone who shares our vision and mission, including individuals from all backgrounds and experience levels in tech and cybersecurity. Our events are also open to everyone, aiming to create an inclusive environment for learning and collaboration. You can volunteer by participating in our annual volunteer recruitment program or applying for temporary positions for specific events. Volunteer opportunities include event organization, content creation, community outreach, social media management, and more, based on your skills and interests.
How can I collaborate for a project or share my story with the WTS community? ➕
To collaborate, contribute content, or share your story with WTS, contact us via email at indonesianwts@gmail.com or reach out through our social media channels. We welcome partnerships that align with our mission and values. We also welcome articles, blog posts, and personal stories that inspire and educate our community.
